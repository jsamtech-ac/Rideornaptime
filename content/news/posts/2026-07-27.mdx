Two things happened that hit family budgets this week: Disneyland quietly raised food prices across 178 dining locations (average 8%, some items up 23%), and historical patterns strongly suggest ticket prices will jump around October 7. If you were on the fence about a fall trip, the math just changed. Here's what to do about it.

## 🚨 Trip-Planning Impact

**Food prices went up across the entire resort — plan your budget accordingly.** Disneyland hit 178 dining locations with an average 8% increase, with some individual items up as much as 23% (the Wedge Salad jumped from $11.99 to $14.79). The biggest visible hikes families will notice: ice cream (Clarabelle's single scoop went from $6.79 to $7.49, a 10% jump), churros, popcorn, bottled drinks, and Mickey beignets. Snack budget for a family of four is roughly $15-25 higher per day now than it was in June.

Real moves that work: pack your own snacks (Disneyland allows outside food — this is your highest-leverage move), refill water bottles at any counter-service location for free (also unchanged), and split entrees for kids under 6. If you were already planning one character dining meal per trip, expect that to run $10-15 more per person than it did last summer.

**Ticket prices are almost certainly going up around October 7.** Disney's raised prices on tickets, Lightning Lanes, and Magic Keys on October 8 and October 9 in the last two years respectively — right around the start of their fiscal year. Food increases typically precede ticket increases by 6-10 weeks. If you have a fall or winter trip in mind and haven't booked, the window to lock in current pricing is closing. Get Away Today lets you book now, pay later with interest-free installments — that's the play if cash flow is the concern.

**D23 Expo is August 14-16 — 18 days out.** Disney's biggest fan event happens in Anaheim and is when major Parks announcements typically drop. Waiting until August 17 to book any 2027 trip still makes sense — announcements could reshape what's opening, closing, or worth planning around.

## 🍿 Food Worth Knowing

The Plaza Inn fried chicken meal remains the best sit-down value at Disneyland Park despite the price hikes. Even after the increases, it's still cheaper per person than most character dining options, the sides are kid-friendly, and it doesn't require a reservation.

**Earl of Sandwich** at Downtown Disney continues to be a top quick-service option for picky-eater families. Prices held steady in the post-hike audit — sandwiches are still around $10-12, which is a genuine value at the resort.

**Bopo Go!** (the Jamba replacement at Downtown Disney) is still not open. If your family relies on smoothies for a low-key snack, plan on Nekter, a resort hotel juice bar, or bring your own from Target/Trader Joe's before you enter the parks.

## 👀 What We're Watching

**D23 Day at Disneyland is August 13, day before the main expo.** Disney's been deliberately quiet, teasing "dazzling surprises." Expect at least one big Disneyland-specific reveal. Historical odds strongly suggest this is when they announce a new land, ride, or major renovation project — the Coco land rumor and Avatar-themed land are the two most likely candidates.

**Yzma might be the new Oogie Boogie Bash villain.** Disney dropped a teaser video with beakers and feathers, and fans are convinced it's Yzma from The Emperor's New Groove. If your kid loves the movie, this is a fun coincidence for a Halloween Time visit. Oogie Boogie Bash October dates are largely sold out; August and early September $139 tickets still have availability.

**Southern California is in a heat wave.** Expect afternoon temps in the mid-90s to low-100s for the next 7-10 days. If you're visiting this week or next, plan an aggressive nap break between 1-4 PM at your hotel — not "we'll take it easy in the shade at DCA." Actually leave the park. The Grand Californian pool and Paradise Pier pool are your friends if you're staying on-property.

**October 7 is the date to watch for ticket price hikes.** Not confirmed yet, but it matches the 2024 and 2025 pattern exactly.
