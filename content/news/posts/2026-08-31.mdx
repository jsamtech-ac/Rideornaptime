This is a book-now-or-pay-more week. 2027 vacation packages just opened, the complete Holidays at Disneyland Resort lineup got announced, and the widely-expected October 7 ticket price hike is five weeks out. If you have any 2027 trip in mind — spring break, summer, holidays — the math for locking in current pricing has never been more straightforward. Here's what changes for your trip.

## 🚨 Trip-Planning Impact

**2027 Disneyland vacation packages opened for booking August 25.** Full vacation packages and hotel-only bookings are now live for arrivals January 1 through December 31, 2027. The 499-day booking window means later 2027 dates will unlock gradually throughout the next year. What matters for families: **booking now locks in current pricing** before the almost-certain October 7 ticket price hike, and it grabs limited Grand Californian and Disneyland Hotel availability before spring break and summer 2027 dates sell out. If you were on the fence about a 2027 trip, the fence just got harder to stay on.

**Holidays at Disneyland Resort — full lineup announced.** Runs November 13, 2026 through January 6, 2027. The family-relevant details:

- **Santa moves to Mickey's Toontown Trolley Barn** (near Roger Rabbit's Car Toon Spin), November 13 through December 24. New location, less-traveled area, which for families with young kids usually means shorter lines. This is a legitimate improvement over last year's Santa's Reindeer Round-Up setup.
- **Bluey's Best Day Ever continues** at Fantasyland Theatre through the holiday season. If you have a Bluey-obsessed toddler, this alone can anchor a whole Disneyland Park day.
- **New character dining options** at Storytellers Cafe: Daisy's Holiday Pajama Party (kids show up in PJs, meet Daisy and friends — designed exactly for the 2-6 crowd) and Mickey's Christmas Carol Feast (better for kids 5+).
- **Haunted Mansion Holiday** carries over from Halloween Time through the holidays with the annual gingerbread house — this year celebrating 25 years of the tradition, taking the form of a music box.
- **"it's a small world" Holiday** returns.
- **Believe... In Holiday Magic fireworks + snowfall on Main Street, it's a small world, and Rivers of America**.
- **World of Color – Season of Light** returns to DCA after skipping 2025 for the 70th anniversary World of Color Happiness.
- **A Christmas Fantasy Parade** returns to Disneyland Park.
- **Mickey's Happy Holidays cavalcade** at DCA.
- **Cars Land holiday overlays**: Mater's Jingle Jamboree and Luigi's Joy to the Whirl.

**Silly Symphony Swings finally reopened August 28.** After being closed since April 27 — nearly 4 months — the classic DCA swings are back. Not trip-changing, but if your kid loves swings and you'd rearranged plans around this closure, you're clear.

**Kids' Summer Tickets expire September 7 — one week left.** Kids 3-9 visit for as low as $50/day. This deal will not return until next summer. If you have any window between now and September 7, book this week.

## 🎃 Halloween Time — One Week In

Halloween Time has been running a week now. Early reports from the last week: Plaza de la Familia at DCA is landing exactly as hoped for families with young kids — Miguel meet-and-greets have been easy walkups on weekdays, Coco music sets are running in Paradise Gardens Park throughout the afternoon. Halloween Screams fireworks runs Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays only during Halloween Time — if you're visiting mid-week, plan on a projection-only version without fireworks.

The Nightmare Before Christmas Collection is officially in wide release across Disney Store and the parks. Popcorn buckets are already going in and out of stock. If you specifically want a 2026 seasonal bucket, buy on your first day.

## 🍿 Food Worth Knowing

Halloween Time seasonal food across 150+ items continues to run through October 31. **Little Red Wagon corn dogs** with the seasonal churro variants remain the highest-quality kid-friendly seasonal pick. **Alien Pizza Planet** is the reliable young-kid lunch option in Tomorrowland.

**Reminder: Minnie & Friends Breakfast at Plaza Inn is still closed through late September.** Substitutes: Goofy's Kitchen at Disneyland Hotel (Halloween-themed through October 31), or wait for the new holiday character dining options that open November 13.

**Bopo Go!** at Downtown Disney: still not open.

## 👀 What We're Watching

**October 7 ticket price hike** is the number to circle. No official Disney confirmation yet — but the pattern has held two years running (October 8, 2024 and October 9, 2025). Multi-day tickets, Park Hopper add-ons, Magic Keys, and parking are all likely to be affected.

**Tiana's Bayou Adventure opens at Disneyland November 15** — timed with the start of Holidays at the Disneyland Resort. 40-inch height requirement. For families with kids 4-7 who loved Splash Mountain, the theming is warmer and less intense.

**Oogie Boogie Bash October dates are almost entirely sold out.** Late September nights still have $139 availability.

**Mickey's Toontown Trolley Barn Santa location** starts November 13. This is a genuine upgrade over prior years for families with young kids — new location, less foot traffic, easier stroller maneuvering.

**Coco ride construction progress** continues at DCA near Paradise Pier. No opening date yet, but Disney says work is actively underway on the show building.
