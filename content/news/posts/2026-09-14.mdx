Three stories with real trip-planning impact this week. Mad Tea Party closes today for what looks like a substantial refurbishment (no reopen date), Pixar Place Hotel pool is completely down for the next 2 weeks (huge if you have a booking), and Minnie & Friends Breakfast returns September 20 after nearly 5 weeks off. Oogie Boogie Bash is officially sold out for 2026. Here's what changes.

## 🚨 Trip-Planning Impact

**Mad Tea Party closes today, September 14 — no reopen date announced.** The classic Alice-in-Wonderland spinning teacups ride goes down for refurbishment, and Anaheim public permits suggest this is a substantial job (not a routine closure). Multiple sources now show the ride closed into October at minimum, with real possibility of a longer closure. For families with young kids: Mad Tea Party is one of the few Fantasyland rides that works for almost any age, and there's no direct substitute. Casey Jr. and Storybook Land Canal Boats are your low-stimulation Fantasyland plays while it's down. If a teacup ride was on your kid's must-do list, plan without it for the next 4+ weeks and check back.

**Pixar Place Hotel pool is completely closed for the next 2 weeks — this is a real disruption.** The Pixel Pool and spa are closed September 8-19. Crush's Surfin' Slide and the Nemo splash pad are closed September 8-25. Disney is shuttling Pixar Place guests to other resort pools: Disneyland Hotel today, Grand Californian September 15-19. For families who specifically booked Pixar Place because of the rooftop pool (Crush's Surfin' Slide is one of the marketed reasons to stay there), this is a genuine downgrade. If you have a booking during this window, either accept the shuttle situation, ask Disney to move you to a different resort hotel, or push your trip to late September at minimum. If you're booking new, avoid Pixar Place through September 25.

**Minnie & Friends Breakfast in the Park returns September 20.** After nearly 5 weeks off, the popular character breakfast at Plaza Inn accepts reservations again. If you canceled a trip specifically because of this closure, or if this was on your kid's must-do list, you're clear starting September 20. Reservations are already open — book now if this matters, because September 20-27 will be tight availability the day it comes back.

## 🎃 Halloween Time — Week 4

Halloween Time is now in its fourth week. What's landing well for families with young kids based on the past three weeks of reports:

- **Plaza de la Familia at DCA continues to be the underrated standout.** Miguel meet-and-greets are still relatively short waits on weekdays. Coco music sets in Paradise Gardens Park run through the afternoon.
- **Mickey and Friends Halloween Cavalcade** at Disneyland has been drawing steady crowds — grab a spot 10-15 minutes early on weekends.
- **Halloween Screams fireworks** continues to run Friday, Saturday, and Sunday nights only. If fireworks matter, plan a weekend visit.
- **Fantasmic! is weekend-only in September.** This wasn't well-flagged in Disney's communications. If your kid was looking forward to Fantasmic! on a weekday, they're out of luck this month.

**Oogie Boogie Bash officially sold out for 2026.** The only remaining option is the D23 Gold Member-exclusive event on Sunday September 27 (must be a paid D23 Gold member). If you're a Gold Member and haven't booked, this is your last shot. The $7,500 VIP Tour is also technically available on sold-out nights, but that's not practical family math.
