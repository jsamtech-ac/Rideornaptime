The season transition is happening in real time this week. Halloween Time has 3 days left and the parks are at peak Halloween crowds, "it's a small world" closes Friday and reopens November 13 as its Holiday overlay (right as Holidays season begins), the Haunted Mansion Holiday 25th anniversary lenticular trading card series launched Monday for Magic Key holders, and Holidays at the Disneyland Resort officially opens November 13 (16 days away). Indiana Jones Adventure is also back in rotation with improved efficiency. Here's what changes for your trip.

## 🚨 Trip-Planning Impact

**Halloween Time ends Friday, October 31.** Last day of overlays, character costumes, Halloween Time food, Mickey and Friends Halloween Cavalcade, and Halloween Screams fireworks. If you have any flexibility and haven't been yet, Thursday October 29 is the single best remaining day (less crowded than Friday or Halloween weekend itself). Halloween weekend will hit 7 to 8 out of 10 crowd levels, with Saturday Halloween night being the most crowded day of the season. Haunted Mansion Holiday is the one exception that stays open past October 31, continuing through early January as part of the Holidays season rotation.

Final Halloween Screams fireworks: Thursday October 29, Friday October 30, Saturday October 31. If fireworks are the point, Halloween night is the obvious call but comes with the crowd tradeoff.

**"it's a small world" closes Friday October 30 and reopens November 13 as its Holiday overlay.** The ride's last regular-version day is October 29. It closes October 30 through November 12, then reopens November 13 as "it's a small world" Holiday — right as Holidays at Disneyland Resort begins. So there's essentially no gap in availability: families visiting on or after November 13 get the Holiday version (worth timing a trip around — the Holiday overlay is beautiful and runs through early January). The only families who need to plan around this are those who specifically want the regular, non-holiday version — ride it before October 30, because once it reopens it stays in Holiday form for the season.

**Indiana Jones Adventure is back and running better.** Returned October 22 after refurbishment. Disney swapped hydraulic actuators on the temple doors for electrical ones, which means the opening scene doors are more reliable and the ride can dispatch more vehicles per hour. Expect lower waits than pre-refurb. Height requirement is still 46 inches (older kids only, roughly 7 and up), but worth re-adding to any late-2026 or 2027 trip plan if you have an older kid who was disappointed by its absence.

**Holidays at Disneyland Resort opens Thursday, November 13.** Full programming kicks in across both parks, hotels, and Downtown Disney. 16 days away. Full breakdown in the "What's Coming" section below.

## 🎃 Halloween Time, Final 3 Days

Plaza de la Familia at DCA runs 2 extra days past Halloween Time (through November 2), so Sunday November 1 and Monday November 2 are the final chances to experience Miguel, the Coco music sets, and the authentic food at Paradise Gardens Park. If you can't make it to Halloween Time proper this year, these 2 bonus days are genuinely worth the trip for Coco families.

Monday October 26 kicked off the Haunted Mansion Holiday 25th Anniversary Lenticular Trading Card and Frame Display for Magic Key holders. New card drops weekly through the end of the year, 10 cards total in the collection, each featuring a different portrait from the ride. First week's card comes with a frame. If you're a Magic Key family with a kid who collects, start now and build the set over the remaining weeks.

Mickey and Friends Halloween Cavalcade at Disneyland and Mickey's Trick and Treat at DCA both wrap October 31.

Oogie Boogie Bash events continue on scheduled nights through October 31. DCA closes early (typically 6 PM) to general-admission guests on event nights. Final event nights: October 28 (tonight), 30, and 31.

## ❄️ Holidays at Disneyland Resort Opens November 13

The complete programming for Holidays at Disneyland Resort is confirmed. Here's what matters for families with kids 2 to 8:

**Santa at two locations.** Santa greets guests November 13 through December 24 at two spots: Mickey's Toontown Trolley Barn (new location this year, near Roger Rabbit's Car Toon Spin at Disneyland) and the Redwood Creek Challenge Trail at DCA (classic spot). The Toontown location is the better pick for families with young kids: less-traveled area of the park, shorter lines than Redwood Creek based on past years, and easier stroller maneuvering.

**A Christmas Fantasy Parade** returns to Disneyland Park. Daytime parade. One of the best daytime entertainment moments for the 2 to 8 crowd.

**"Believe...In Holiday Magic" fireworks** run nightly from November 13 through the end of the year. Snowfall effects on Main Street, the "it's a small world" facade (once the ride reopens November 13), and Rivers of America. This is a core Disneyland holiday experience.

**"World of Color: Season of Light"** returns to Paradise Bay at DCA for the first time since 2024 (did not run in 2025). This is worth planning a DCA evening around.

**"Mickey's Happy Holidays" cavalcade** at DCA. New-to-this-year addition at the Buena Vista Street.

**"Mirabel's Gifts of the Season"** debuts at Buena Vista Street (new this year). Mirabel from Encanto in a seasonal format.

**"A Musical Christmas with Mariachi Alegría de Disneyland & Miguel!"** on select evenings at DCA. Miguel from Coco joins the mariachi band for holiday songs. New this year.

**Disney Festival of Holidays at DCA** runs the full season with 6 festival marketplaces featuring new and returning food and drinks. Culturally substantive in the way Plaza de la Familia was during Halloween Time.

**Disney ¡Viva Navidad!** returns at DCA.

**Haunted Mansion Holiday** continues (seamless transition from Halloween Time). This year celebrates 25 years of the overlay with a new gingerbread music box.

**Three holiday character dining experiences** run November 16 through January 3: Goofy's Kitchen Celebrates the Holidays, Mickey's Christmas Carol Feast, and Daisy's Holiday Pajama Party (the last one is specifically designed for the 2 to 6 crowd with kids showing up in pajamas).

**Chip and Dale's Ornament Trail** runs November 11 through January 6 at Downtown Disney (starts 2 days before the main Holidays season).

**Downtown Disney nightly snowfall** starting November 13. Seasonal food, decor, and live entertainment through January 6.

**New merchandise collections**: Mickey Mouse and Family Christmas Collection and Disney Vintage Holiday Collection.

**Characters in new winter outfits**. Mickey and friends get alpine and travel-inspired holiday looks at Disneyland Park; retro-inspired wintry wear at DCA.

## 🎆 Fireworks Transition (November 1 Through 12)

There's a weird 12-day window between Halloween Screams ending and Believe...In Holiday Magic starting. During November 1 through 12, Disneyland runs regular (non-seasonal) nighttime fireworks on select nights. Lower energy, less thematic. If your trip falls in this window and your kid is excited about Disneyland fireworks, set expectations: you're seeing the baseline show, not a seasonal one. The castle will have some snow visible but the parade and Holiday overlays are not yet active.

## 🍿 Food Worth Knowing

**Pumpkins and Pancakes at Naples** at Downtown Disney runs Saturday October 31 as the final date. Pumpkin-themed brunch menu. Reservations recommended but walkups work on last day.

Halloween Time food runs 3 more days. Everything disappears November 1. There's a 12-day gap where park menus are at their generic baseline. Holidays food lands November 13.

**Bluey Playhouse arrived at Downtown Disney at $89.99.** Interactive toy modeled on the Heeler family home. For Bluey families, this is worth knowing about but genuinely overpriced. Comparable Bluey playsets on Amazon run $45 to $65 for similar quality. If your kid asks, buy from Amazon and skip the markup. The Downtown Disney version has no exclusive features beyond being sold at Disneyland.

## 👀 What We're Watching

**Tiana's Bayou Adventure opens November 15** (18 days out). 40-inch height requirement. For families with kids 4 to 7 who loved Splash Mountain, the theming is warmer and less intense.

**Monsters Inc. 2027 closure timing still unconfirmed.** Avatar land groundbreaking suggests mid-to-late 2027. If your kid loves Monsters Inc., prioritize a ride on any 2026 or early 2027 trip.

**Hazel Hightower S.E.A. interactive quest launched.** New Society of Explorers and Adventurers storyline involving a time-traveling thief, the cursed Shiriki Utundu idol, and Harrison Hightower's daughter. Mostly for older kids and adults who like the in-park alternate reality game. Not relevant for families with young kids but worth noting if you have a 10+ year old who's into puzzles.

**Castle snow installation continues.** Started October 12. Still partially complete heading into Halloween weekend, which means visitors over the final weekend will see the unusual sight of Halloween decor on the ground and Holiday decor overhead. Something to prepare younger kids for if they're sensitive to visual chaos.

**Coco ride construction** at DCA continues. Opening still points to 2028 or 2029.

**Anzellan droidsmiths at Droid Depot** still pending install. No date. Over 3 months of "coming soon."

**Disney cost-cutting continues.** No new news this week beyond the ongoing 1,000 confirmed job cuts and operational belt-tightening.
