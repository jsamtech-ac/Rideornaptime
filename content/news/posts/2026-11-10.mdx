Holidays at Disneyland Resort opens Friday, November 13. Three days away. This is the single biggest seasonal transition of the year at the parks, and the full programming kicks in all at once across both parks, hotels, and Downtown Disney. Also two corrections to lead with. Here's the thorough roundup.

## ✍️ Corrections From Prior Roundups

Before the news, two errors I want to fix publicly.

**Tiana's Bayou Adventure at Disneyland is not opening November 15, 2026.** It opened at Disneyland on November 15, 2024, and has been running for 2 years. The ride I was referring to is the Walt Disney World version at Magic Kingdom (currently closed November 2, reopens December 18). I was wrong in several prior weekly roundups. If you were planning a November 15 Disneyland trip around a Tiana's Bayou opening, the ride will be running as it has been since 2024, which means no 40-inch-height-requirement new attraction to work into your plan. On the positive side, Tiana's Bayou at Disneyland is a solid ride for families with kids 4 to 7 who loved Splash Mountain, with warmer theming and a less intense drop profile.

**"it's a small world" reopens November 13, not November 29.** The closure is 14 days, not 30. The ride reopens as "it's a small world" Holiday on the exact day Holidays season starts. For families with toddlers who were worried about a long gap during Holidays opening weeks, you're clear. The Holiday overlay is beautiful and runs through early January.

## 🚨 Trip-Planning Impact

**Holidays at Disneyland Resort opens Friday, November 13.** This is the trip-planning headline of the fall. Full programming lands simultaneously across both parks. For families with young kids, the Friday November 13 opening day will be the single most crowded weekday of the month because it combines Holidays season launch with a Friday arrival. If your trip is that specific day, expect 7 to 8 out of 10 crowd levels. Saturday and Sunday November 14 to 15 will be similar. The Monday-to-Thursday window of the following week (November 16 to 19) is the sweet spot for experiencing the full Holidays season with much lower crowds.

**Santa arrives at Mickey's Toontown Trolley Barn starting Friday, November 13.** New location this year, near Roger Rabbit's Car Toon Spin. Open through December 24. The Toontown location is genuinely the better spot for families with young kids compared to the Redwood Creek Challenge Trail Santa at DCA. Less-traveled area of the park, easier stroller maneuvering, and historically shorter lines than the DCA alternative. If Santa is a priority for your kid, rope-drop Disneyland on your Toontown day and head straight there.

**Tomorrowland Skyline Terrace is closed all month, into late January 2027.** This is the elevated viewing space above Tomorrowland that typically hosts the Evening Dining Package for nighttime spectaculars. Not a ride, not something most families with young kids would use, but worth knowing if you booked any Tomorrowland Skyline Terrace dining experience. Those reservations should have been auto-cancelled by Disney. If you had one and haven't been contacted, call Disney Dining to confirm.

**Weekday park hours shortened for November and December (except Thanksgiving week).** Disneyland is continuing the 2025 pattern of shorter weekday hours with later weekend hours. Fridays and Saturdays plus Thanksgiving week run late (Disneyland closes at midnight, DCA at 10 PM). Weekdays close earlier. If you were expecting the old pattern of late nights every night during Holidays season, adjust. The afternoon nap break matters more than ever on weekday visits because the evening window is shorter.

## ❄️ Holidays at Disneyland: What's Live Friday

Everything below starts simultaneously on Friday, November 13.

**A Christmas Fantasy Parade** returns to Disneyland Park daily. One of the best daytime entertainment moments for kids 2 to 8. Afternoon showtime typically between 3:30 and 5 PM.

**"Believe...In Holiday Magic" fireworks** run nightly from November 13 through the end of the year. Snowfall effects on Main Street, "it's a small world" facade, and near Rivers of America. Main Street Station between the castle hub and Town Square is the best viewing spot for families with kids who want to see the castle projections and avoid the fireworks-shadow problem that happens closer to Main Street.

**"it's a small world" Holiday** opens with the Holiday overlay. New narration, holiday music in multiple languages, Christmas theming layered over the existing dolls, and a transformed exterior facade with projection effects.

**"World of Color: Season of Light"** returns to Paradise Bay at DCA. Did not run in 2025. Worth planning a DCA evening around.

**A Christmas Fantasy Parade** (Disneyland) and **"Mickey's Happy Holidays" cavalcade** (DCA Buena Vista Street) both launch.

**"Mirabel's Gifts of the Season"** debuts at Buena Vista Street. Mirabel from Encanto. New this year.

**"A Musical Christmas with Mariachi Alegría de Disneyland & Miguel!"** on select evenings at DCA. Miguel from Coco joins the mariachi band for holiday songs. New this year.

**Disney Festival of Holidays at DCA** runs the full season with 6 festival marketplaces. Culturally substantive like Plaza de la Familia was during Halloween Time.

**Disney ¡Viva Navidad!** returns at DCA.

**Haunted Mansion Holiday** continues (seamless transition from Halloween Time). 25th anniversary gingerbread music box display is the visual highlight.

**Cars Land seasonal overlays:** Mater's Jingle Jamboree and Luigi's Joy to the Whirl.

**Downtown Disney nightly snowfall** starts November 13. Seasonal food, decor, and live entertainment through January 6.

**Chip and Dale's Ornament Trail** at Downtown Disney (started November 11, 2 days before the main Holidays season).

**Characters in new winter outfits.** Mickey and friends get alpine and travel-inspired holiday looks at Disneyland Park, retro-inspired wintry wear at DCA.

## 🍿 Food Worth Knowing

**Seasonal character dining starts November 16** and runs through January 3. Three experiences worth knowing about:

- **Daisy's Holiday Pajama Party** at Storytellers Café, Grand Californian. Kids show up in pajamas and meet Daisy and friends. Specifically designed for the 2 to 6 crowd. This is probably the single best character dining move of the Holidays season for families with young kids. Book fast when reservations open.
- **Mickey's Christmas Carol Feast** at Storytellers Café. Dinner. Better for kids 5 and up.
- **Goofy's Kitchen Celebrates the Holidays** at Disneyland Hotel. Chaotic, loud, kid-friendly. The reliable party-atmosphere pick.

**New holiday food items** roll out resort-wide Friday. Confirmed items worth knowing about: Mickey Train Light-Up popcorn bucket, Holiday Stitch Christmas Tree Sipper (DCA), Milk and Cookies Tree-shaped Macaron at Jolly Holiday Bakery Cafe, merry cranberry crispy chicken sandwich at Galactic Grill.

Alien Pizza Planet, Little Red Wagon corn dogs, and Ronto Wraps at Galaxy's Edge remain the year-round reliable kid-friendly picks.

**Bopo Go!** at Downtown Disney: still not open. Over 3 months of visible construction with no announced date. At this point, it would not surprise me if this doesn't open until early 2027.

## 👀 What We're Watching

**Thanksgiving week (November 23 through 29) will be the most crowded week of November and December.** If your trip is in that window, expect 8 to 9 out of 10 crowd levels across both parks. If you have any flexibility, the week before (November 16 to 22) and the week after (November 30 to December 6) are both significantly lower-crowd windows with the same Holidays programming.

**Monsters Inc. 2027 closure timing still unconfirmed.** Avatar land groundbreaking suggests mid-to-late 2027. Ride on any 2026 or early 2027 trip if your kid loves Monsters Inc.

**Anzellan droidsmiths at Droid Depot** still pending install. This has been "coming soon" since August. Fair to assume now that this is a 2027 install at the earliest.

**Disney cost-cutting continues** across the parks. Visible operational and entertainment belt-tightening through the Holidays season. If something feels thinner compared to prior Holidays trips, you are not imagining it.

**Castle snow installation is mostly complete.** The castle and Small World facade have both transitioned to Holiday looks ahead of Friday's opening.
