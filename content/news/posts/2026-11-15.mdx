Holidays at Disneyland opened Friday November 13 and the first weekend wrapped today. Everything major landed on schedule. The biggest draw this year is the return of World of Color Season of Light at DCA (skipped 2025 for the 70th), a new Guardians of the Galaxy holiday dance off that most families don't know about yet, Santa at the new Toontown Trolley Barn location, and 400,000 lights on "it's a small world" Holiday. Also a practical reminder: opening day was rainy. Thanksgiving week is 8 days away. Here's what to plan around.

## 🚨 Trip-Planning Impact

**Holidays opening weekend went as scheduled.** All major programming launched Friday November 13: A Christmas Fantasy Parade, Believe...In Holiday Magic fireworks nightly, World of Color Season of Light at DCA, "it's a small world" Holiday reopened, Santa at Mickey's Toontown Trolley Barn opened, Cars Land holiday overlays activated, and Downtown Disney nightly snowfall started. Everything the press release promised is now operating. No major opening-weekend hiccups beyond rain on opening day (more on that below).

**World of Color Season of Light is the biggest draw this year.** The holiday version of the nighttime water show skipped 2025 for the 70th anniversary celebration and is back for the first time in two years. 25-minute show at Paradise Bay featuring scenes from Beauty and the Beast, Inside Out, Frozen, Cinderella, and other holiday-themed Disney animation. For families planning a DCA evening, this is the single best nighttime experience available right now. The show hits capacity quickly (roughly 4,500 viewing spots per show), so either join the virtual queue the morning of your visit or book the Season of Light Dessert Party for guaranteed premium viewing. Standard viewing works for families willing to arrive 60 to 90 minutes early.

**A new Guardians of the Galaxy holiday dance off is running at Avengers Campus.** This is underreported. The overlay plays select times at Avengers Campus during Holidays season. For families with Marvel-fan kids 6+, it's a fun addition to a DCA day. Check the Disneyland app the morning of your visit for showtimes. Opening weekend visitors got to see it first, but it runs through January 6.

**Opening day (November 13) was rainy and the monorail got stuck.** Not a trip-canceling story, but a practical reminder: November and December in Anaheim can be genuinely wet. Average rain probability in Anaheim doubles in November compared to October. The monorail (which has no air conditioning or heat and sits exposed) got stuck Friday during the rain. If you're visiting this season, pack real ponchos (not the cheap in-park ones), waterproof shoes for yourself, and a change of socks for kids in a backpack. Strollers with rain covers save your trip. The park runs in rain and most rides keep operating (outdoor rides like Big Thunder and Matterhorn may temporarily close in heavy weather).

**Thanksgiving week (November 23 through 29) is 8 days out.** This is the most crowded week of November and December at Disneyland with crowd levels of 8 to 9 out of 10 forecast across the week. If your trip falls that window, expect long waits and plan aggressively for midday nap breaks. Thanksgiving Day itself (Thursday November 26) is usually one of the slightly lower-crowd days of the week because many families are home eating, so hit rope drop that morning. Black Friday (November 27) and the weekend are at peak.

## 🎄 Holidays Season: First-Weekend Reports

Based on the first weekend of Holidays, here's what families should know.

**Santa at Mickey's Toontown Trolley Barn is working as hoped.** Lines ran shorter than Redwood Creek Challenge Trail on opening weekend. The Toontown location is genuinely easier to navigate with a stroller and the less-traveled area means less crowd pressure on young kids. If Santa is on your list, Toontown is the pick. Both locations run through December 24.

**A Christmas Fantasy Parade** runs daytime at Disneyland. Daily. Opening weekend showtimes landed around 3:30 and 5:30 PM. Grab a spot 15 minutes early for families with young kids who need front row. For the 2 to 8 crowd, this is the top daytime entertainment of Holidays season.

**"Believe...In Holiday Magic" fireworks** runs nightly. Snowfall effects on Main Street, "it's a small world" Holiday facade, and along Rivers of America. Main Street Station between the castle hub and Town Square is the best viewing spot. Avoid the Main Street crush if you have a stroller.

**"it's a small world" Holiday reopened with 400,000 lights on the facade.** The Holiday overlay is genuinely one of the best seasonal transformations at Disneyland. Worth planning around.

**World of Color Season of Light at DCA, nightly 9 PM.** 25 minutes. Beauty and the Beast and Inside Out are the headline film tags this year. Reserved viewing via virtual queue (available in the app the morning of your visit) or the dessert party is the move if you have a kid who can't stand 60 minutes.

**Buena Vista Street Tree Lighting** happens nightly at DCA. 50-foot tree. Lower-key than fireworks, works well for young kids who get overwhelmed by the main nighttime shows.

**The Three Caballeros street party** is part of Disney ¡Viva Navidad! at DCA on select days. Mickey's Happy Holidays cavalcade runs through Buena Vista Street. The Holiday Toy Drummers appear on select days.

**Chip and Dale's Ornament Trail** at Downtown Disney (started November 11). Interactive scavenger-hunt style walk with Chip and Dale themed ornaments. Works for kids 4+ who can follow a map.

## 🍿 Food Worth Knowing

**Holiday character dining starts tomorrow, Monday November 16, and runs through January 3.** Three experiences:

- **Daisy's Holiday Pajama Party** at Storytellers Café, Grand Californian. Kids show up in pajamas and meet Daisy and friends. This is designed for the 2 to 6 demographic specifically. Book immediately via the Disneyland app, reservations are opening now and filling fast.
- **Mickey's Christmas Carol Feast** at Storytellers Café. Dinner. Better for kids 5 and up.
- **Goofy's Kitchen Celebrates the Holidays** at Disneyland Hotel. Chaotic, loud, kid-friendly. The reliable party-atmosphere pick.

**New holiday food items** rolled out resort-wide Friday. Confirmed worth-trying items: Mickey Train Light-Up popcorn bucket, Holiday Stitch Christmas Tree Sipper at DCA, Milk and Cookies Tree-shaped Macaron at Jolly Holiday Bakery Cafe, merry cranberry crispy chicken sandwich at Galactic Grill.

**Disney Festival of Holidays marketplaces at DCA** opened Friday across 6 locations. The food is genuinely good and culturally substantive. If you're doing a DCA day, plan to eat across the marketplaces rather than commit to a single sit-down.

Alien Pizza Planet, Little Red Wagon corn dogs, and Ronto Wraps at Galaxy's Edge remain the year-round reliable kid-friendly picks.

**Bopo Go!** at Downtown Disney: still not open. Over 3 months of visible construction.

## 👀 What We're Watching

**Thanksgiving week (November 23 to 29) crowds forecast at 8 to 9 out of 10.** If you have any flexibility, the week before (November 16 to 22) and the week after (November 30 to December 6) are both significantly lower-crowd windows with the same Holidays programming.

**World of Color Season of Light demand will spike through Thanksgiving week.** Opening weekend had reserved viewing fill within the first hour of park opening. For Thanksgiving week visits, either join the virtual queue the second the app allows it or book the dessert party as a guarantee.

**Monsters Inc. 2027 closure timing still unconfirmed.** Avatar land groundbreaking suggests mid-to-late 2027. Ride on any 2026 or early 2027 trip if your kid loves Monsters Inc.

**Coco ride construction** at DCA continues. Opening still points to 2028 or 2029.

**Anzellan droidsmiths at Droid Depot** still pending install. Over 4 months of "coming soon" now. Reasonable to assume this is a 2027 install.

**Rain in the 10-day forecast.** November and December in Anaheim include real rain probability. Pack for it. The expensive in-park ponchos are not necessarily better than what you can bring from home.

**Disney cost-cutting continues** across the parks. Visible operational and entertainment belt-tightening through the Holidays season. If something feels thinner compared to prior Holidays trips, you are not imagining it.
