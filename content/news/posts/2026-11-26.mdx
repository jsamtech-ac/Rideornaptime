## 🚨 Trip-Planning Impact

**Tomorrow (Friday November 27) is the worst crowd day of November and December.** Every forecast puts Black Friday at 9 to 10 out of 10 at Disneyland, higher than Thanksgiving Day itself and competitive with Christmas week peak days. If you're already committed to a tomorrow visit, strategy matters more than ever: rope-drop by 7:45 AM, use Lightning Lane Multi Pass aggressively (buy pre-arrival if you haven't), plan a hard midday nap break at the hotel from noon to 3 PM, and focus on 2 or 3 high-value attractions rather than trying to hit everything. If your trip is flexible, move to Saturday November 28 or Sunday November 29, which both forecast slightly lower (8 to 9 out of 10) as day-trippers go home.

**Black Friday is also the best ticket deal day of the year.** Get Away Today, Undercover Tourist, and other Disney-authorized third-party sellers typically release their deepest discounts of the year on Black Friday and run them through Cyber Monday (November 30). For families with a 2027 trip in mind, tomorrow through Monday is the window to buy. Expect Get Away Today to layer additional savings on top of their standard Disneyland packages. These deals do not apply to 2026 travel (prices are locked), but any 2027 arrival date is eligible.

## 🎄 Holidays Season: Settling Into Rhythm

Two weeks into Holidays and the season is in full swing. Based on this week's observations:

**World of Color Season of Light** continues to be the top nighttime draw. Virtual queue slots fill within the first 30 to 60 minutes of park opening. For Thanksgiving weekend visitors (including anyone going tomorrow or Saturday), join the virtual queue immediately at park open or book the Season of Light Dessert Party as a guaranteed premium viewing option.

**Santa at Mickey's Toontown Trolley Barn** has been running 30 to 60 minute waits during Thanksgiving week versus 45 to 90 minute waits at Redwood Creek Challenge Trail. Toontown remains the better pick for families with young kids. Both locations close December 24.

**A Christmas Fantasy Parade** at Disneyland running daytime, typically 3:30 PM show. Grab a spot 20 to 30 minutes early on Black Friday and Saturday.

**"Believe...In Holiday Magic" fireworks** runs nightly. Main Street Station between the castle hub and Town Square is the best viewing spot for families with strollers. During Thanksgiving weekend, stake out your spot 60 minutes before.

**"it's a small world" Holiday** continues to be the top daytime ride for young kids. Expect 45 to 75 minute waits during Thanksgiving weekend, dropping back to 20 to 35 minutes starting Sunday November 29.

**Candlelight Processional is December 5 and 6.** 50-year tradition where a celebrity narrator reads the Nativity story over a mass choir performance on Main Street. Beautiful and unique. Also drives meaningful crowds those two days (6 to 7 out of 10 forecast). If you're visiting that weekend, plan to catch it or plan to work around it.

## 🍿 Food Worth Knowing

**Thanksgiving Day dining happened today.** Napa Rose, Carthay Circle, Storytellers Café, and Steakhouse 55 all ran special Thanksgiving menus. Most of these are already past, but check for day-of walk-in availability if you're at the resort right now.

**Holiday character dining continues** through January 3. Daisy's Holiday Pajama Party at Storytellers Café remains the best pick for families with kids 2 to 6. Thanksgiving weekend availability is gone. December 1 through 13 reservations are tight but available if you check daily for cancellations.

**Disney Festival of Holidays marketplaces at DCA** continue across 6 locations. The Mariachi Tostada with pulled pork and the Pozole Verde are the two items worth planning around.

Alien Pizza Planet, Little Red Wagon corn dogs, and Ronto Wraps at Galaxy's Edge remain the year-round reliable kid-friendly picks.

**Bopo Go!** at Downtown Disney: still not open.
